> 💡 **Arabic tip:** Arabic has **only a definite article (ال)** and **no indefinite article**. In Arabic, *"كتاب"* = *a book*, and *"الكتاب"* = *the book*. So the English `a/an` has no Arabic equivalent, and learners often forget it!

## 2. Part A: Indefinite Articles `a` / `an` | أداة التنكير

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
Articles define a noun as **specific** or **unspecific**.
> *I'm **a** nurse. **The** hospital I'm working in is huge.*

### 🔤 `a` or `an`? Look at the SOUND, not the spelling | الحكم للصوت لا للحرف

| Use | When the next word starts with… | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | a **consonant sound** | **a** table, **a** university /juː/ |
| **an** | a **vowel sound** | **an** umbrella, **an** honest person /ɒ/ |

> ⚠️ **Trap words:**
> - `university` starts with the letter *u* but the sound **/juː/** (a consonant sound) → **a** university
> - `honest` / `hour` start with letter *h* but it is **silent** → **an** honest person, **an** hour

### 🎯 When to use `a/an`

| # | Use | الاستعمال | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A **general idea**, not a particular thing | فكرة عامة | What does **a** fox say? |
| 2 | Listener **doesn't know which** one | غير معروف للمستمع | Helen's brother works in **a** factory. |
| 3 | **First mention** (new information) | أول ذكر | I bought **a** new computer. |
| 4 | **Jobs** | المهن | I'm **a** nurse. / She is **an** English teacher. |

## 3. Part B: Definite Article `the` | أداة التعريف

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
`the` limits a noun to **one particular thing**, and it is used when it's **clear which one** we mean.
> ***The** cake is in the fridge. I know that Kate made it.*

### 🔁 First mention vs. second mention | المرة الأولى والثانية

> I bought **a** new computer. *(1st time → new info)*
> **The** computer is much better than my old one. *(2nd time → listener knows)*

### ✅ We use `the` with… | نستعمل the مع

| # | Category | الفئة | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | Nationalities & groups | الجنسيات والفئات | **the** French, **the** Italians, **the** old, **the** poor |
| b | Time expressions | تعابير الزمن | in **the** past, in **the** future *(but: **at present**, no article)* |
| c | Superlatives (& ordinals) | صيغة التفضيل | **the** worst trip, **the** first one |
| d | Musical instruments | آلات موسيقية | I played **the** piano |
| e | Plural / group countries | دول بصيغة جمع أو اتحاد | **the** U.S., **the** U.K., **the** United Arab Emirates, **the** Netherlands |
| f | Names of ships | أسماء السفن | We sailed on **the** Claudia |
| g | Oceans | المحيطات | **the** Pacific, **the** Atlantic |
| h | Rivers | الأنهار | **the** Amazon, **the** Nile |

> 💡 **Extra tip (teacher):** `the` is also used for **seas** (*the Mediterranean Sea*), **mountain ranges** (*the Alps*) and **unique things** (*the sun, the moon*). Single countries, cities and lakes usually take **no article** (*Tunisia, Paris, Lake Geneva*).

---

## 4. Part C: Zero Article `–` | بدون أداة

Use **no article** with **plural** and **uncountable** nouns when talking **in general**.

| General (no article) | Specific (the) |
|---|---|
| **Dogs** are not allowed in that shop. *(all dogs)* | **The dogs** next door were barking. *(those dogs)* |
| **Love** is a wonderful thing. | **The love** of my parents helped me. |
| **Rabbits** are small mammals. | **The rabbits** in my garden are white. |

> 💡 **Extra zero-article cases:** *at present, on foot, go home, at night*, languages (*I speak English*), and meals (*have breakfast*).

---

## 5. 🔍 Quick Decision Tree | شجرة القرار السريعة

```
Is the noun SPECIFIC (both speaker & listener know which one)?
   ├─ YES → use  THE
   └─ NO  → Is it singular & countable?
              ├─ YES → use  A / AN   (a = consonant sound, an = vowel sound)
              └─ NO (plural / uncountable, general meaning) → NO ARTICLE ( – )
```

## 7. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
a   + consonant SOUND   (a table, a university)
an  + vowel SOUND       (an umbrella, an honest person, an hour)

a/an → first mention • any one • general idea • jobs
the  → specific/known • 2nd mention • superlatives • unique things
       nationalities (the French) • groups (the poor) • instruments (the piano)
       plural countries (the U.S., the Netherlands) • oceans • rivers • ships
 –   → plural/uncountable in GENERAL (Dogs…, Love…) • at present • on foot
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| She is **an** English teacher. | She is ~~a~~ English teacher. |
| I play **the** piano. | I play ~~piano~~. |
| **Love** is wonderful. | ~~The love~~ is wonderful *(in general)*. |
| It's **a** university. | It's ~~an~~ university. |
| She's not working **at present**. | She's not working ~~at the present~~. |
| We flew over **the** Atlantic. | We flew over ~~Atlantic~~. |

## 8. Practice with Answers | تمارين مع الحلول

**Fill in the gaps with `a`, `an`, `the` or `–` (no article).**

| # | Sentence | Answer & Reason |
|---|---|---|
| a1 | ___ dog that bit me ran away. | **The**: specific dog (the one that bit me) |
| a2 | ___ wounds were treated by ___ doctor. | **The … the**: specific wounds & doctor |
| a3 | ___ Dr. Smith is ___ professional! | **– … a**: titles/names take no article; `a` + job/quality |
| b1 | ___ Rabbits are ___ small mammals in ___ family Leporidae.* | **– … – … the**: general plural; "the family" is specific |
| c1 | ___ Love is ___ wonderful thing. | **– … a**: uncountable in general; `a` + singular noun |
| d1 | ___ Olivia is ___ English teacher. | **– … an**: name has no article; English = vowel sound → an |
| d2 | She's not working at ___ present. | **–**: fixed expression *at present* |
| e1 | I didn't want to go to ___ parking lot. | **the**: a specific, known place |
| e2 | …I went ___ home on ___ foot. | **– … –**: *go home*, *on foot* are fixed expressions |
| e3 | It was only ___ 15 minute walk. | **a**: "fifteen" starts with a consonant sound |

> \* **Teacher's note:** The correct spelling of the rabbit family is **Leporidae** (the worksheet has "Leporadae"). Also, a compound adjective like *15-minute* is normally hyphenated: *a 15-minute walk*.
