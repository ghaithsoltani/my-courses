## 2. Part A: What do they have in common? | ما المشترك؟

Both `each` and `every`:
- describe **members of a group** | يصفان أفراد المجموعة
- are used **only with countable nouns** | مع الأسماء المعدودة فقط
- are placed **before the noun** | قبل الاسم
- are followed by a **singular noun** and a **singular verb** | اسم مفرد وفعل مفرد
- are often **interchangeable** | غالبًا يتبادلان

> ***Each** person is unique.* = ***Every** person is unique.*

> 💡 **Arabic tip:** Arabic uses **"كل"** for both. English separates them: `each` = *كل واحد على حدة*, `every` = *كل (الجميع)*.

---

## 3. Part B: `EACH` (كل واحد على حدة)

### 📌 Meaning | المعنى
`each` **highlights the individual** member of a group (one by one).
> ***Each** book on the shelf had a unique cover.*

### 📊 Patterns of `each` | أنماط each

| # | Pattern | النمط | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **each + singular noun** | مع اسم مفرد | **Each book** had a unique cover. |
| 2 | **each of + the/plural noun** | مع of + جمع | **Each of the pupils** received a card. |
| 3 | **each of + plural pronoun** | مع of + ضمير | **Each of us** got a ticket. |
| 4 | **subject + each + verb** | بعد الفاعل | My siblings **each** have their own room. |
| 5 | **… + each** (end of sentence) | في آخر الجملة | Mom gave us $20 **each**. (= to each of us) |

> ⚠️ **Rule:** with plural nouns/pronouns, `each` **must** be followed by **of** → *each **of** the pupils*, never ~~each the pupils~~.
> ⚠️ **Verb:** *Each **of the pupils received***: the verb is **singular** after "each of" (formal). But after the subject: *My siblings each **have*** (plural verb, because the subject is plural).

---

## 4. Part C: `EVERY` (كل / كافة)

### 📌 Meaning | المعنى
`every` sees the group as a **collection of individual members** (all of them, no exception).

### 📊 Patterns of `every` | أنماط every

| # | Pattern | النمط | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **every + singular noun** | مع اسم مفرد | **Every boy** in my class wanted that game. |
| 2 | **every + time expression** | مع الزمن | **Every morning** Phillip goes for a run. |
| 3 | **every time** | في كل مرة | And **every time** Ann would forgive him. |

> ❌ **`every` CANNOT be used with plural nouns:**
> ~~Every boys~~ → ✅ **Every boy**
> ❌ `every` cannot be used **after the subject** or **at the end** of a sentence (only `each` can).
> ❌ `every` is not followed by `of` directly. Use **every one of** (*every one of the pupils*).

---

## 5. 🔍 Each vs. Every: Side by Side | مقارنة سريعة

| | **each** | **every** |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | individual (one by one) | whole group (all, no exception) |
| + singular noun | ✅ each book | ✅ every book |
| + plural noun | ✅ **each of the** books | ❌ ~~every books~~ |
| + pronoun | ✅ each **of us** | ❌ ~~every of us~~ |
| After subject | ✅ We **each** have a room. | ❌ |
| End of sentence | ✅ $20 **each** | ❌ |
| Time expressions | possible, less common | ✅ **every** day / time |
| Group size | 2 or more | usually 3 or more |

## 7. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
EACH  = one by one (individual focus)
   each + SINGULAR noun          each book
   each OF + plural noun/pronoun each of the pupils / each of us
   subject + each + verb         My siblings each have…
   … + each (end)                $20 each

EVERY = all, no exception (group focus)
   every + SINGULAR noun ONLY    every boy
   every + time                  every morning / every time

RULES: singular noun • countable only • "every" never with plural • "each" needs OF + plural
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| **Every boy** wanted it. | ~~Every boys~~ wanted it. |
| **Each of the pupils** got a card. | ~~Each the pupils~~ got a card. |
| We paid $500 **each**. | We paid $500 ~~every~~. |
| **Every** morning I run. | ~~Each of~~ morning I run. |
| **Each of us** was late. | ~~Every of us~~ was late. |

## 8. Practice with Answers | تمارين مع الحلول

### Exercise 1: Choose the correct distributive

| # | Sentence | Answer | Why? |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | My two sisters ___ have their own business. | **each** | after the subject → only `each` |
| b | …we are paying $500 ___. | **each** | at the end → only `each` |
| c | ___ of the board directors signed the papers. | **Each** | followed by `of` → `each of` |
| d | …to ___ his own. | **each** | fixed idiom: *to each his own* |
| e | ___ day I read 20 pages. | **Every** (or Each) | time expression → `every` is more common |

### Exercise 2: Rewrite with the word in brackets

| # | Original | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| a | Whenever I tell you to calm down, you snap at me. (every) | **Every time** I tell you to calm down, you snap at me. |
| b | Susan, Meghan, Kelly and I got free tickets. (each) | **Each of us** got free tickets to the cinema. |
| c | The police searched all the buildings… (every) | The police searched **every building** in the neighbourhood. |
| d | Different people like different things. (each) | **To each his own.** *(or: Each person likes different things.)* |
| e | …all of our friends were trying to reach you too! (each) | **Each of our friends was** trying to reach you too! |

> **Teacher's notes:**
> - In the intro, the worksheet says *"gave my sister and **I** $20 each"*. Since `my sister and I` is the **object** here, the correct form is **"my sister and **me**"** (see Chapter 2 → object pronouns).
> - In 2d, the key gives the idiom *"To each his own"*. A more literal rewrite is *"Each person likes different things."* Both are fine.
> - Modern English often prefers **"to each their own"** (gender-neutral).
