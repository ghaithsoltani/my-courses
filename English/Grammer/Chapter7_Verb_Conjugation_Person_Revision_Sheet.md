## 2. Part A: What is Conjugation? | ما هو التصريف؟

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
**Verb conjugation** = how a verb **changes** to show a different **person, number, tense, or mood**.
التصريف هو تغيّر شكل الفعل ليدل على الشخص والعدد والزمن والصيغة.

> ***I'm** a student.* → 1st person • singular • present simple • indicative mood

### 📊 The Persons | الأشخاص

| Person | الشخص | Singular (مفرد) | Plural (جمع) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st** | المتكلم | **I** (أنا) | **we** (نحن) |
| **2nd** | المخاطب | **you** (أنتَ/أنتِ) | **you** (أنتم/أنتن) |
| **3rd** | الغائب | **he, she, it** (هو/هي) | **they** (هم/هن) |

### 🎯 Examples | أمثلة

| Person | Example |
|---|---|
| 1st | **We** work on Saturdays. |
| 2nd | **You** need to take a break. |
| 3rd | **It** is snowing outside. |
| 3rd (noun) | **Sarah** has signed up for a yoga class. *(Sarah = she)* |

> 💡 **Trick:** To find the person, **replace the subject with a pronoun**.
> *The Potts* → **they** → 3rd person plural.
> *These jeans* → **they** → 3rd person plural.
> *Sarah* → **she** → 3rd person singular.

> ⭐ **Extra tip (teacher):** in the present simple, **3rd person singular** adds **-s/-es**: *I work, you work, **he works***, *she watch**es***. All other persons keep the base form.

## 3. Part B: The Verb `to be` | فعل الكينونة

`to be` is **irregular**: **3 forms** in the present (am / is / are) and **2 forms** in the past (was / were).

### 📊 Conjugation Table | جدول التصريف

| Person | **Present** (الحاضر) | | **Past** (الماضي) | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | Singular | Plural | Singular | Plural |
| **1st** | I **am** | we **are** | I **was** | we **were** |
| **2nd** | you **are** | you **are** | you **were** | you **were** |
| **3rd** | he/she/it **is** | they **are** | he/she/it **was** | they **were** |

### ✂️ Short forms (contractions) | الصيغ المختصرة
| Full | Short |
|---|---|
| I am | **I'm** |
| you are / we are / they are | **you're / we're / they're** |
| he is / she is / it is | **he's / she's / it's** |
| are not / is not | **aren't / isn't** |
| was not / were not | **wasn't / weren't** |

### 🧩 Other forms of `be` | صيغ أخرى
| Form | Name | Used after / in |
|---|---|---|
| **be** | base form | after modals: *You must **be** kidding me.* |
| **been** | past participle | after have/has: *We have **been** here for ages.* |
| **being** | -ing form | continuous: *He is **being** silly.* |

> 🌍 **Important Arabic tip:** In Arabic, the verb "to be" is usually **omitted** in the present (*أنا طالب*). In English you **must** include it: *I **am** a student* ✅, ~~I a student~~ ❌.

## 4. 🔍 Quick Memory Aid | مساعد الحفظ

```
I      → am  / was
he, she, it (and any singular noun) → is / was
you, we, they (and any plural noun) → are / were   (you → ALWAYS are/were)
```

> ⚠️ `I was` ✅ but `you was` ❌ → **you were**.
> ⚠️ `we was` ❌ → **we were**.

## 6. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
PERSONS:   1st: I / we     2nd: you     3rd: he, she, it / they

TO BE      PRESENT                PAST
  I        am                     was
  you      are                    were
  he/she/it is                    was
  we       are                    were
  they     are                    were

Find the person: replace the subject with a pronoun (The Potts → they).
Present simple: only he/she/it adds -s (he works).
Arabic speakers: don't forget "am/is/are" in English!
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| I **am** a teacher. | I ~~is~~ a teacher. / ~~I a teacher~~ |
| You **were** late. | You ~~was~~ late. |
| They **are** friends. | They ~~is~~ friends. |
| She **is** tired. | She ~~are~~ tired. |
| We **were** at home. | We ~~was~~ at home. |
| You must **be** kidding. | You must ~~is~~ kidding. |

| # | Sentence | Answer | Why? |
