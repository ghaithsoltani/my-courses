## 2. Part A: Proper Nouns | أسماء العلم

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
A **proper noun** names a **unique** person, place, or thing. It is **always capitalized**.
اسم العلم هو اسم يدل على شيء **فريد** (شخص، مدينة، شركة…) ويُكتب **دائمًا بحرف كبير**.

> *London is the capital of **Great Britain**.*
> *You can buy coffee at **Starbucks**.*

### ✅ What must be capitalized? | ماذا نكتب بحرف كبير؟

| Category | الفئة | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Names of people | أسماء الأشخاص | **Olivia** |
| Cities, countries, continents | مدن، دول، قارات | **Europe**, **New York** |
| Planets | كواكب | **Mars** |
| Companies / brands | شركات وعلامات تجارية | **Starbucks**, **Coca-Cola** |
| Festivals & holidays | الأعياد والمناسبات | **Christmas**, **Thanksgiving** |
| People's titles (before a name) | ألقاب الأشخاص | **President** Trump |
| Books, films, plays, paintings | كتب، أفلام، مسرحيات، لوحات | *The Old Man and the Sea* |

### 🎯 Special Rules | قواعد خاصة

**1) Titles of works**: capitalize the **nouns, adjectives, and verbs** (small words like *and, the, of* stay lowercase unless first).
عناوين الأعمال: نكتب الأسماء والصفات والأفعال بحرف كبير.
> *Pride and Prejudice*

**2) A person's name = their work.**
اسم الشخص قد يعني أعماله.
> *We were listening to **Mozart**.* (his music)
> *I'm reading an **Iris Murdoch**.* (her book)

**3) Family words (mom, dad, uncle, aunt)**: capitalize **only when used as a name** (addressing the person directly).
كلمات العائلة: حرف كبير فقط إذا استُعملت **كاسم**.

| ✅ Used as a name (Capital) | ❌ Not a name (small letter) |
|---|---|
| Please ask **Dad** if he can… | Is your **dad** coming over? |
| My **aunt Kelly** *(aunt = small, Kelly = name)* | My **mom** and **dad** got me a gift. |

> 💡 **Trick:** Replace the word with a real name (e.g., *Ahmed*). If it still works → capital letter.

**4) Proper noun vs. proper adjective**
| Proper Noun (اسم علم) | Proper Adjective (صفة) |
|---|---|
| **Asia** is a continent. | I don't like **Asian** food. |

Both are capitalized, but check the **function** of the word.

## 3. Part B: Common Nouns | الأسماء العامة

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
A **common noun** names a **class** of things or a concept, not a particular individual.
الاسم العام يدل على **فئة** أو مفهوم عام وليس على فرد محدد.

> *There was a **sofa**, two **chairs**, and a **wardrobe** in the **room**.*

### ⚠️ Capitalization | الحروف الكبيرة
Common nouns are **NOT capitalized**, unless they **begin a sentence** or are **part of a title**.
> **Capitals** of countries are usually large cities. *(first word)*
> London is the **capital** of Great Britain. *(middle of sentence → small)*

## 4. Forming Plurals | تكوين الجمع

| # | Rule | القاعدة | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Add **-s** (most nouns) | نضيف **s** | flower → flower**s**, dog → dog**s** |
| 2 | Ends in **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z, -o** → add **-es** | نضيف **es** | bus → bus**es**, watch → watch**es**, box → box**es**, potato → potato**es** |
| 3 | Ends in **consonant + -y** → change **y → i** + **-es** | نغيّر y إلى ies | baby → bab**ies**, lady → lad**ies** |
| 4 | Ends in **-f / -fe** → often change to **-ves** | غالبًا f ← ves | life → li**ves**, wolf → wol**ves**, thief → thie**ves** |
| 5 | Exceptions to rule 4 | استثناءات | belief → belief**s**, chef → chef**s** |

> 💡 **Extra notes (teacher's tips):**
> - Rule 3 applies only after a **consonant**: *baby → babies*, but vowel + y just adds -s: *boy → boys*, *day → days*.
> - Rule 2 (-o) has exceptions: *photo → photos*, *piano → pianos*.

## 6. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
PROPER NOUN  → unique → ALWAYS Capital   (Tunis, Mozart, Christmas)
COMMON NOUN  → general → small letter    (city, music, holiday)

Capital also for : festivals • titles before names • books/films/paintings
Family words     : Capital only if used as a NAME (Dad! / my dad)
Plurals          : +s | +es (s,ss,sh,ch,x,z,o) | y→ies | f/fe→ves
Irregular        : man-men, woman-women, person-people, child-children,
                   tooth-teeth, foot-feet, mouse-mice
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| Your **feet** are cold. | Your ~~foot~~ are cold. |
| Many **thieves** here. | Many ~~thiefs/thiefes~~ here. |
| I like **jeans**. | I like ~~jeanses~~. |
| Take your **jacket**. | Take your ~~Jacket~~. |
| She is a **doctor**. | She is a ~~Doctor~~. *(but: **Doctor** Smith)* |
