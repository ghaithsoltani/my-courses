## 2. Part A: The Basic Idea | الفكرة الأساسية

The verb must **agree** with its subject in **person** and **number**.
الفعل يجب أن يتوافق مع الفاعل في الشخص والعدد.

> **Liz is** an accountant and **she has** a typical 8–5 job.
> **She cooks** dinner, and **her brothers make** breakfast.

| Subject | Verb (to be) |
|---|---|
| I | **am** |
| he / she / it / singular noun | **is** |
| you / we / they / plural noun | **are** |

> 💡 In the present simple: **singular subject → verb + s** (*she cook**s***), **plural subject → base verb** (*they cook*).

---

## 3. Part B: The 8 Rules | القواعد الثماني

### Rule 1: `and` → PLURAL | العطف بـ and = جمع
Two or more subjects joined by **and** take a **plural verb**.
> **Brothers and sisters don't** often get along.
> A car **and** a bike **are** my means of transportation.

### Rule 2: Singular indefinite pronouns → SINGULAR | ضمائر مفردة
These are **always singular**: `each, each one, either, neither, everyone, everybody, anyone, anybody, nobody, somebody, someone, no one`.
> **Each of these suggestions is** interesting.
> **Someone was standing** at the door.
> **Nobody masters** a language without making mistakes.

### Rule 3: `or / nor` with singular subjects → SINGULAR | أو / ولا
> **Either your mother or dad needs** to contact me.

### Rule 4: Proximity: mixed singular + plural with `or/nor` | قاعدة القرب
The verb agrees with the subject **closest to the verb**.
> The teacher or **the students write** homework on the board. *(students = plural, closest)*
> The students or **the teacher writes** homework on the board. *(teacher = singular, closest)*
> Neither Sarah nor **I am** going to college. *(I is closest → am)*

### Rule 5: `There is / There are` | هناك
`there` is **not** the subject → the verb agrees with **what follows**.
> **There is** a book on the table. *(singular)*
> **There are** books on the table. *(plural)*

### Rule 6: Portions: a lot of, some, all, a majority | الكميات
The verb agrees with the **noun after `of`**.
> **There is a lot of fuss** around his arrival. *(fuss = singular)*
> **There are a lot of people** in the room. *(people = plural)*

### Rule 7: Distance, time, money = ONE unit → SINGULAR | وحدة واحدة
> **Ten dollars is** a high price for socks. *(the amount)*
> But: **Ten dollars were** scattered on the floor. *(separate bills)*
> **Ten kilometers is** too far to walk.

### Rule 8: Collective nouns → SINGULAR | أسماء الجمع
`family, group, team, committee, class, jury, staff…`
> **My family is** very big.
> **The group meets** every other week.
> **The jury has** reached a decision.

> 💡 **Teacher's note:** In **British English**, collective nouns can take a plural verb when you think of the **individual members** (*The team **are** arguing*). In American English, singular is the norm. The worksheet follows the singular rule.

## 4. 🔍 Quick Decision Table | جدول القرار السريع

| Subject looks like… | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A **and** B | **plural** | Tom and Ann **are** here. |
| A **or** B (both singular) | **singular** | Tom or Ann **is** here. |
| everyone / nobody / each / someone | **singular** | Everyone **is** here. |
| a lot of + **plural** noun | **plural** | A lot of people **are** here. |
| a lot of + **singular/uncountable** noun | **singular** | A lot of money **is** needed. |
| collective noun (team, family) | **singular** | The team **is** ready. |
| amount of money/time/distance | **singular** | Five minutes **is** enough. |
| There + noun | agrees with the **noun** | There **are** two books. |

## 6. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
and          → PLURAL        (Tom and Ann ARE)
or / nor     → CLOSEST noun  (Tom or the kids ARE / the kids or Tom IS)
everyone, nobody, each, someone, no one … → SINGULAR
there is/are → verb agrees with the noun AFTER it
a lot of / some / all + of + noun → verb agrees with that noun
money, time, distance (as a unit) → SINGULAR
collective nouns (team, family, jury) → SINGULAR (BrE: sometimes plural)
"as well as", "together with" do NOT make the subject plural:
   I, as well as my friends, AM excited.
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| My cousins and my brother **don't** cook. | My cousins and my brother ~~doesn't~~ cook. |
| **Nobody masters** a language. | Nobody ~~master~~ a language. |
| Ten kilometers **is** too far. | Ten kilometers ~~are~~ too far. |
| **These** jeans **are** revealing. | ~~This~~ jeans ~~is~~ revealing. |
| The group **meets** weekly. | The group ~~meet~~ weekly. |
| There **are** many people. | There ~~is~~ many people. |

## 7. Practice with Answers | تمارين مع الحلول

| # | Sentence | Answer | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | The aim of the researches **was** to find life on Mars. | **was** | subject = *aim* (singular), not *researches* |
| b | The group **meets** every other week. | **meets** | collective noun |
| c | My cousins and my brother **don't** know how to cook. | **don't** | `and` → plural |
| d | Nobody **masters** a language without mistakes. | **masters** | `nobody` is singular |
| e | Neither Sarah nor I **am** going to college. | **am** | proximity: closest = *I* |
| f | I, as well as my friends, **am** excited. | **am** | subject = *I* (*as well as* doesn't add subjects) |
| g | The jury **has** finally reached a decision. | **has** | collective noun (BrE also accepts *have*) |
| h | Ten kilometers **is** too far to walk. | **is** | distance as a unit |
| i | **These** jeans **are** too revealing. | **These … are** | *jeans* is plural |
| j | A car and a bike **are** my means of transportation. | **are** | `and` → plural |

> **Teacher's note:** the worksheet sentence (a) has small typos: *"researches"* should be **research**, and *"life of Mars"* should be **life on Mars**.
