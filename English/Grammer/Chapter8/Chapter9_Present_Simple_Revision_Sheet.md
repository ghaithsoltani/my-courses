## 3. Part B: Forming the Present Simple | التكوين

### ✅ Positive (+) | المثبت
**Base verb** (bare infinitive). Add **-s / -es** only for **he / she / it**.

| Person | Example |
|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | I **work**, you **work**, we **work**, they **work** |
| he / she / it | he **works**, she **works**, it **works** |

### ✏️ Spelling rules for he/she/it | قواعد الإملاء

| Rule | القاعدة | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Most verbs → **+s** | | like → like**s**, work → work**s** |
| Ends in **o, s, ch, sh, x** (also z) → **+es** | | watch → watch**es**, do → do**es**, go → go**es**, miss → miss**es** |
| **Consonant + y** → **y → ies** *(extra)* | | study → stud**ies**, cry → cr**ies** |
| Vowel + y → **+s** *(extra)* | | play → play**s** |

### 🔀 Irregular verbs | الأفعال الشاذة

| `to be` | | `to have` | |
|---|---|---|---|
| I **am** | we **are** | I **have** | we **have** |
| you **are** | you **are** | you **have** | you **have** |
| he/she/it **is** | they **are** | he/she/it **has** | they **have** |

> 🌍 **BrE vs AmE:** *(BrE)* I **have got** a car. / *(AmE)* I **have** a car. (same meaning)

### ❌ Negative (−) and ❓ Question (?) | النفي والسؤال

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **(−)** I/you/we/they | subject + **do not (don't)** + base verb | I **don't use** the Internet. |
| **(−)** he/she/it | subject + **does not (doesn't)** + base verb | He **doesn't get up** at 6. |
| **(?)** I/you/we/they | **Do** + subject + base verb? | **Do** you **know** the time? |
| **(?)** he/she/it | **Does** + subject + base verb? | **Does** he **get up** at 6? |
| **Wh-?** | Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb? | **When does** he **get up**? |

> ⚠️ **Golden rule:** with `does / doesn't` the main verb has **NO -s** (the -s is already on *does*).
> ✅ *He doesn't **use** it.* ❌ ~~He doesn't uses it.~~
> ✅ *Does she **work**?* ❌ ~~Does she works?~~

## 4. ⏰ Adverbs of Frequency | ظروف التكرار

| Word | العربية | % |
|---|---|---|
| always | دائمًا | 100% |
| usually | عادةً | ~80% |
| often | غالبًا | ~60% |
| sometimes | أحيانًا | ~40% |
| rarely | نادرًا | ~10% |
| never | أبدًا | 0% |
| every day / week / year | كل يوم / أسبوع / سنة | |

### 📍 Position | الموقع
| Case | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Normal verbs | **BEFORE** the verb | I **usually cook** at home. / She **often stays** home. |
| Verb `to be` | **AFTER** the verb | Kim **is always** late. / My sister **is always** in a hurry. |
| With negatives | after *don't* is not common → use before | I **don't always** get their jokes. |

## 6. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
(+)  I/you/we/they + V          He/she/it + V+s/es
(−)  I/you/we/they + don't + V   He/she/it + doesn't + V
(?)  Do + I/you/we/they + V ?    Does + he/she/it + V ?

-es after: o, s, ch, sh, x (z)      consonant+y → -ies
TO BE: am / is / are       TO HAVE: have / HAS

Adverbs: BEFORE normal verbs  |  AFTER "to be"
   I always eat…              |  She is always late
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| She **watches** TV. | She ~~watch~~ TV. |
| He **doesn't use** it. | He ~~doesn't uses~~ it. |
| **Do you know** the time? | ~~Does you know~~ the time? |
| My sister **is always** in a hurry. | My sister ~~always is~~ in a hurry. |
| What time **do you go** to bed? | What time ~~you go~~ to bed? |
| She **has** a car. | She ~~have~~ a car. |
