## 2. Part A: When do we use `will`? | متى نستعمل will؟

| # | Use | الاستعمال | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Predictions** | توقعات | **Perhaps** it'll snow tomorrow. |
| 2 | **Beliefs & opinions** | معتقدات وآراء | Things **will happen** on their own. |
| 3 | **Hopes & promises** | آمال ووعود | I promise I **will do** all my assignments on time. |
| 4 | **Spontaneous decisions / offers** *(extra)* | قرارات لحظية وعروض | Why can't you come? **I'll let** you sleep at my place. / **I'll pay** for the meal. |

> ℹ️ English also expresses the future with **be going to** (plans) and **present tenses** (timetables). This chapter focuses on **will**.

---

## 3. Part B: Forming the Future Simple | التكوين

**will + base verb (infinitive without *to*)**, the same for **all persons**.

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **(+) Positive** | subject + **will** + base verb | Sam **will** probably **move** to Canada. |
| **(−) Negative** | subject + **will not (won't)** + base verb | Sam **won't move** to Canada. |
| **(?) Question** | **Will** + subject + base verb? | **Will** Sam **move** to Canada? |
| **(?) Wh-question** | Wh-word + **will** + subject + base verb? | **Where will** Sam **move** to? |

### ✂️ Contractions | الاختصارات
| Full | Short |
|---|---|
| I will | **I'll** |
| you will | **you'll** |
| he / she / it will | **he'll / she'll / it'll** |
| we will | **we'll** |
| they will | **they'll** |
| will not | **won't** |

> ⚠️ After `will`: **no -s, no -ing, no to**.
> ✅ *She will **go**.* ❌ ~~She will goes / will going / will to go.~~

### How sure? | ما درجة اليقين؟
| Word | العربية | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| definitely / certainly | بالتأكيد | 🔵🔵🔵 very sure |
| probably | على الأرجح | 🔵🔵 |
| perhaps / maybe | ربما | 🔵 not sure |

### 📍 Position | الموقع
| Case | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | adverb **AFTER `will`** | We'll **probably** do it tomorrow. |
| Negative | adverb **BEFORE `won't`** | We **probably** won't do it tomorrow. |
| `perhaps` | usually at the **start** | **Perhaps** you'll make the right decision. |

## 5. `shall`: suggestions | shall للاقتراح

`shall` is used with **I / we** for **suggestions and offers**. As a pure future it's old-fashioned and rare.
> **Shall I** go or **shall we** leave together?
> **Shall we** go to the restaurant tonight?

## 7. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
(+)  subject + will + V        She will come / She'll come
(−)  subject + won't + V       She won't come
(?)  Will + subject + V ?      Will she come? / When will she come?

Same for ALL persons • V = base form (no s / ing / to)
Adverbs: will PROBABLY…  |  PROBABLY won't…
shall → only for suggestions: Shall I / Shall we …?
Uses: predictions • beliefs • hopes • promises • quick decisions
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| She **will come**. | She ~~will comes~~. |
| He **won't go**. | He ~~won't to go~~. |
| **Will** you **help** me? | ~~Do you will~~ help me? |
| We'll **probably** do it. | We'll do ~~probably~~ it. |
| We **probably won't** do it. | We won't ~~probably~~ do it. |
| **Shall we** go? | ~~Shall you~~ go? |

## 8. Practice with Answers | تمارين مع الحلول

### Exercise 1: Find the time markers
| # | Sentence | Time markers |
|---|---|---|
| a | **Perhaps** you'll make the right decision. | **Perhaps** |
| b | Max will **certainly** come **later tonight**. | **certainly**, **later tonight** |
| c | Susan will try to go there **next month** but it **probably** won't happen. | **next month**, **probably** |

### Exercise 2: Write the correct sentence
| # | Answer | Why? |
|---|---|---|
| a | I**'ll let** you sleep at my place. | spontaneous offer |
| b | I **won't do** it unless you prove Jane wrong. | negative |
| c | We**'ll try** to make it work. We**'ll let** you know. | promise |
| d | **Shall we go** to the restaurant tonight? I**'ll pay** for the meal. | suggestion (`shall we`) + offer |

### Exercise 3: Sample answers
- a. I'll probably stay at home and sleep the whole day.
- b. I'll stay at my friends' place.
- c. Robots won't take over the planet. / I don't think that robots will take over the planet.

> **Teacher's note:** In English we usually say **"I don't think robots will…"** rather than ~~"I think robots won't…"~~. It sounds more natural.

## 9. Self-Test (30 seconds) | اختبر نفسك

1. Make negative: *"They will win."* → **They won't win.**
2. Make a question: *"She will call."* → **Will she call?**
3. Fix: *"I will to go."* → **I will go.**
4. Order: *"probably / we'll / go"* → **We'll probably go.**
5. Order: *"won't / he / probably / come"* → **He probably won't come.**
6. Which is a suggestion? *"Shall we dance?"* or *"Will we dance?"* → **Shall we dance?**
