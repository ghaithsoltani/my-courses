## 2. Part A: When do we use it? | متى نستعمله؟

| # | Use | الاستعمال | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An action at a **definite time** in the past | حدث في وقت محدد انتهى | Shakespeare died **in 1616**. |
| 2 | The action is **finished** | الحدث منتهٍ | I **lived** in Paris. *(I don't now)* |
| 3 | **How someone felt** | مشاعر في الماضي | I **was** beyond happy to marry the love of my life. |
| 4 | **Past habits** (+ every day…) | عادات ماضية | I **ran** every day when I **was** a teen. |

## 3. Part B: Forming the Past Simple | تكوين الماضي البسيط

### ✅ Regular verbs | الأفعال المنتظمة
**All persons have the SAME form** (I / you / he / she / it / we / they).
كل الضمائر لها نفس الصيغة.

| Rule | القاعدة | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Add **-ed** | نضيف ed | work → work**ed**, want → want**ed** |
| Ends in **-e** → add **-d** | ينتهي بـ e نضيف d | like → like**d**, live → live**d** |
| **Consonant + y** → **y → ied** | y تصبح ied | cry → cr**ied**, try → tr**ied** |
| **1 vowel + 1 consonant** (stressed) → **double** the consonant | نضاعف الحرف الأخير | stop → sto**pped**, regret → regre**tted** |
| Vowel + y → just add **-ed** *(extra)* | | play → play**ed**, enjoy → enjoy**ed** |

> 💡 **Teacher's note:** The worksheet says "change -y to **-ie**". The correct ending is **-ied** (*cried, tried*).
> 💡 No doubling when the stress is on the **first** syllable: *visit → visit**ed***, *open → open**ed***.

### 🔊 Pronouncing **-ed** (extra)
| Sound | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **/ɪd/** | after **t / d** | want**ed**, need**ed** |
| **/t/** | after voiceless sounds | work**ed**, stopp**ed**, watch**ed** |
| **/d/** | after voiced sounds | play**ed**, lov**ed**, call**ed** |

## 4. Part C: Negatives & Questions | النفي والسؤال

### 📊 The Pattern | النمط

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **(+) Positive** | subject + **past form** | His sister **lived** in Sutton. |
| **(−) Negative** | subject + **did not (didn't)** + **base verb** | His sister **did not live** in Sutton. |
| **(?) Question** | **Did** + subject + **base verb**? | **Did** his sister **live** in Sutton? |
| **(?) Wh-question** | Wh-word + **did** + subject + **base verb**? | **Where did** his sister **live**? |

> ⚠️ **Golden rule:** after `did / didn't` the main verb goes **back to the base form** (no -ed, no irregular past).
> ✅ *Did you **go**?* ❌ ~~Did you went?~~
> ✅ *She didn't **invite** Ann.* ❌ ~~She didn't invited Ann.~~

### 🎯 Special cases | حالات خاصة

| Verb | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **to have** | uses `did / didn't` | I **didn't have** enough money. |
| **to be** | **NO `did`** → use was/were | **Were you** angry? / I **wasn't** angry. |

## 7. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
(+)  subject + V2            She worked / He went / They were
(−)  subject + didn't + V1   She didn't work / He didn't go
(?)  Did + subject + V1 ?    Did she work? / Where did he go?

REGULAR : +ed | +d (e) | y→ied | double (stop→stopped)
IRREGULAR: memorize (go-went, eat-ate, see-saw …)
TO BE   : was / were  (NO did!)      TO HAVE : did you have / didn't have

AFTER did/didn't → base verb !!!
Markers: yesterday • last night • ago • in 1616 • when
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| Kim **went** on a date. | Kim ~~goed~~ on a date. |
| We **went** to high school. | We ~~wented~~ to high school. |
| I **drove** to Houston. | I ~~driven~~ to Houston. |
| How **did** you **get** the job? | How ~~you did get~~ the job? |
| We **didn't invite** Ann. | We ~~didn't invited~~ Ann. |
| There **were** so many people. | There ~~was~~ so many people. |
| Where **were** you? | Where ~~did you were~~? |
