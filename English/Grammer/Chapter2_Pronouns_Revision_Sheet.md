## 2. Part A: Subject Pronouns | ضمائر الفاعل

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
A **subject** is the person or thing that **performs the action**. A **subject pronoun** takes the place of a noun that is the subject.
ضمير الفاعل يحل محل الاسم الذي يقوم بالفعل في الجملة.

> ***She** told me about her worries.*

### 📊 The Table | الجدول

| Person | الشخص | Singular (مفرد) | Plural (جمع) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | المتكلم | **I** (أنا) | **we** (نحن) |
| 2nd | المخاطب | **you** (أنتَ/أنتِ) | **you** (أنتم/أنتن) |
| 3rd | الغائب | **he** (هو) / **she** (هي) / **it** (هو/هي لغير العاقل) | **they** (هم/هن) |

> 💡 **Note:** `you` is the same for singular and plural. `I` is **always capital**.

### 🎯 When to use each | متى نستعمل كل ضمير؟

| Pronoun | Use for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **he** | a male | *Kyle is looking for a job. **He** …* |
| **she** | a female | *Mrs. Smith is my neighbour. **She** …* |
| **it** | objects, things, animals, ideas | *Love is eternal. **It** will last forever.* |
| **it** | time, weather, temperature | *What time is **it**? **It's** 7 o'clock. **It's** cold today.* |
| **it** | a baby when sex is unknown* | *Their baby is so small. **It** weighs 2 kilos.* |
| **they** | plural people or things | *My kids love books. **They** …* |
| **we** | me + other(s) | *My friends and I like trips. **We** …* |

> \* **Teacher's note:** Using `it` for a baby is acceptable in the worksheet, but many speakers prefer **he/she/they** because `it` can sound impersonal. Likewise, for a pet you know well, **he/she** is common.

### ✂️ Avoid Repetition | تجنّب التكرار
- ~~Mary is a student and Mary is very hard-working.~~
- ✅ Mary is a student and **she** is very hard-working.

## 3. Part B: Object Pronouns | ضمائر المفعول

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
**Object pronouns** replace nouns that are the **direct or indirect object**, the **receiver** of the action.
ضمائر المفعول تحل محل الاسم الذي **يستقبل** الفعل.

> *She told **me** about her worries.*

### 📊 Subject → Object Table | جدول التحويل

| Subject (فاعل) | Object (مفعول) | العربية | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **me** | أنا ← ني/ي | She called **me**. |
| you | **you** | أنتَ ← ك | I love **you**. |
| he | **him** | هو ← ه | I saw **him**. |
| she | **her** | هي ← ها | He likes **her**. |
| it | **it** | هو/هي ← ه/ها | I've seen **it**. |
| we | **us** | نحن ← نا | They invited **us**. |
| they | **them** | هم ← هم | Ask **them**. |

### 📍 Where do object pronouns go? | موقع ضمير المفعول
Object pronouns come **after a verb** or **after a preposition**.

| After a verb (بعد الفعل) | After a preposition (بعد حرف الجر) |
|---|---|
| Ethan asked **me** to talk… | …talk to **them**. |
| I love **them**! | She is in love with **him**. |

### ⚠️ Important Points | نقاط مهمة

**1) `it` looks the same as subject and object.**
> *'Pretty Lady'? **It** is my favourite!* → subject
> *I've seen **it** many times.* → object

**2) "He and I" vs. "her and me"**: use the **subject form** for subjects and the **object form** for objects.
| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| ~~He and me~~ went to the movies. | **He and I** went to the movies. |
| Mrs. Keith called ~~her and I~~. | Mrs. Keith called **her and me**. |

> 💡 **Trick:** Remove the other person. *"~~He and~~ I went"* ✅ / *"~~He and~~ me went"* ❌. *"Mrs. Keith called me"* ✅ / *"called I"* ❌.

**3) Replace repeated objects with object pronouns.**
> I can't stop thinking about Amy. I can't stop imagining my future with ~~Amy~~ **her**.

## 4. 🔍 Subject vs. Object: Side by Side | مقارنة سريعة

| | Subject Pronoun | Object Pronoun |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | Does the action | Receives the action |
| **Position** | **Before** the verb | **After** the verb / preposition |
| **Forms** | I, you, he, she, it, we, they | me, you, him, her, it, us, them |
| **Example** | **They** invited us. | We invited **them**. |

## 6. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
SUBJECT (does the action)      OBJECT (receives the action)
I      →  me                   he   →  him      we   →  us
you    →  you                  she  →  her      they →  them
it     →  it

SUBJECT pronoun  → BEFORE the verb       (She called.)
OBJECT pronoun   → AFTER verb/preposition (called her / with her)

"it" = things, animals, ideas, time, weather, temperature
Compound: "He and I went" / "called her and me"  (remove the other person to test)
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| **She** told **me**. | ~~Her~~ told ~~I~~. |
| Ask **them**. | Ask ~~they~~. |
| It is **us**. / They invited **us**. | They invited ~~we~~. |
| **It** is raining. | ~~They~~ are raining. |
