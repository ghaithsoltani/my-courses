# 💼 The Road to Nexora
## A Story That Teaches English Grammar (Chapters 1 → 11)
### Setting: IT · CEO · Big Companies | القصة: تقنية المعلومات · المدير التنفيذي · الشركات الكبرى

> 👨‍🏫 **How to read this story (للقارئ):** Read the story first. The **bold words** are the grammar points. After each part there is a **Teacher's Box** that explains the rule in English and Arabic. At the end there is a map of the 11 chapters and a final test.

# 📘 PART 1: The Letter (Chapter 1: Nouns)

It was **December**, and **Christmas** lights were shining in the streets of **Tunis**. Yasmine, an IT **engineer**, was sitting in her room with her laptop. On the screen there was an email from **Nexora**, a famous company that competes with **Microsoft**, **Google** and **Amazon**.

*"Dear Ms. Ben Salah, **CEO** Laura Bennett would like to meet you on Monday."*

Yasmine jumped from her chair. "**Dad!** **Mom!** Come here!" she shouted.

Her **dad** ran into the room. "What happened?"

"The **CEO** of Nexora wants to meet **me**!"

Her **mom** smiled. "My daughter will work with the great **engineers** of the world!"

Her brother Adam came in with a book under his arm: ***Pride and Prejudice***. "Are you reading **Mozart** again?" he joked, because Yasmine always listened to **Mozart** while she coded. (She was listening to his **music**.)

That night Yasmine prepared her bag. She put in **two laptops**, a few **photos** of her projects, and some **notes**. She also remembered the lessons of her old teacher: *"In every company there are **men** and **women**, **children** at the company party, **ladies** in the meeting, **thieves** of data, and **lives** that change because of technology. Remember the **taxes**, the **feet** that walk the corridors and the **teeth** that smile for the photo!"*

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 1: Nouns | الأسماء

| Rule | القاعدة | Example from the story |
|---|---|---|
| **Proper noun** = unique name → ALWAYS Capital | اسم العلم بحرف كبير | **Tunis, Nexora, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Christmas, December, Mozart** |
| **Common noun** = general → small letter | الاسم العام بحرف صغير | engineer, company, laptop, music |
| Title **before a name** → Capital | اللقب قبل الاسم | **CEO** Laura Bennett, **Ms.** Ben Salah |
| Titles of works → Capital | عناوين الأعمال | ***Pride and Prejudice*** |
| A person's name can mean his/her work | اسم الشخص = أعماله | "listening to **Mozart**" = his music |
| Family words: Capital **only as a name** | كلمات العائلة | "**Dad!**" ✅ (a name) / "her **dad** ran" ✅ (not a name) |

**Plurals used in the story:**

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| + **s** | laptops, notes, engineers |
| + **es** (s, ss, sh, ch, x, z, **o**) | taxes, (potatoes) |
| consonant + y → **ies** | ladies, (babies) |
| f / fe → **ves** | thieves, lives, (wolves) |
| exceptions | photos, pianos, chefs, beliefs |
| **Irregular** | man → **men**, woman → **women**, child → **children**, foot → **feet**, tooth → **teeth**, person → **people**, mouse → **mice** |

> ⚠️ Not: ~~thiefs~~, ~~jeanses~~, ~~foot are cold~~.

# 📗 PART 2: The Phone Call (Chapter 2: Pronouns)

Yasmine called her best friend **Nour**. "Hello, Nour? **I** have big news. **She** (the CEO) wants to meet **me**!"

"**You** are so lucky!" said Nour. "Is **it** a real interview?"

"Yes, **it** is! **It** is on Monday at 9 o'clock. **It**'s a cold morning, but **it**'s going to be an important day."

Nour asked, "Who is going with **you**?"

"**Adam and I** are taking the train. **He** wants to see the building, and **I** need **him** for the moral support. **We** will leave early."

"Did you tell your parents?"

"Yes, I told **them**. Mom called **her** friends and Dad called **his** boss. **They** were so proud of **us**!"

After the call, Yasmine thought about her future: "I can't stop thinking about the CEO. I imagine my future with **her**."

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 2: Pronouns | الضمائر

| Subject (does the action) | Object (receives the action) |
|---|---|
| I | **me** |
| you | **you** |
| he | **him** |
| she | **her** |
| it | **it** |
| we | **us** |
| they | **them** |

- **Subject pronoun → BEFORE the verb:** *She wants to meet me.*
- **Object pronoun → AFTER a verb or a preposition:** *I need **him**.* / *with **you**.*
- **"It"** = things, animals, ideas, **time, weather, temperature**: *It is 9 o'clock. It is cold.*
- **Avoid repetition:** ~~Yasmine is an engineer and Yasmine is smart.~~ → Yasmine is an engineer and **she** is smart.
- **Compound test:** *Adam **and I** are taking the train* ✅ (remove Adam → "I am taking") / *Mom called **Dad and me*** ✅ (remove Dad → "called me").
- ❌ ~~Him and me went~~ → ✅ **He and I** went.

# 📙 PART 3: On the Train (Chapter 3: Articles)

On Monday morning, Yasmine and Adam took **a** train to the capital. Yasmine bought **a** coffee at Starbucks and Adam bought **an** orange juice. **The** coffee was hot, and **the** juice was cold.

"Tell me about Nexora," said Adam.

"Nexora is **a** university of ideas," Yasmine laughed. "It is **an** honest company with **an** hour of free training every day for each worker."

On the train, a man was playing **the** guitar. Next to him, **an** old woman read about **the** Nile and **the** Pacific. "**The** old always have the best stories," said Adam.

Yasmine remembered **the** worst trip of her life: a flight over **the** Atlantic, delayed for ten hours! "That was **the** worst day ever," she said. "But **the** past is the past. **In the future** I will travel more."

"Do you want to work in **the** U.S. or **the** U.K.?" Adam asked.

"I don't know. **The** Netherlands also has great IT companies. **Dogs** and **cats** don't care about borders, but **engineers** do!" She laughed. "**Technology** changes **lives**."

She wasn't working **at present**, so she had time. She wanted to go to the building **on foot** from the station. It was only **a** 15-minute walk.

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 3: Articles | أدوات التعريف والتنكير

**a / an → look at the SOUND, not the spelling**

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| **a** + consonant sound | **a** train, **a** coffee, **a** university /juː/ |
| **an** + vowel sound | **an** orange juice, **an** honest company, **an** hour (silent h) |

**When to use `a / an`:** first mention • any one • general idea • jobs (*I'm **an** engineer*).

**When to use `the`:** specific / known • 2nd mention • superlatives (**the** worst) • nationalities/groups (**the** old, **the** French) • instruments (**the** guitar) • plural countries (**the** U.S., **the** U.K., **the** Netherlands) • oceans (**the** Atlantic, **the** Pacific) • rivers (**the** Nile) • ships • unique things (**the** sun).

**Zero article ( – ):** plural/uncountable in general (*Dogs, Technology, Love*) • fixed expressions: **at present, on foot, go home, at night**.

> 🧭 **Decision tree:** Specific? → **the**. Not specific + singular countable? → **a/an**. Not specific + plural/uncountable? → **no article**.
> 🌍 **Arabic tip:** Arabic has only "ال". English **a/an** has no Arabic equivalent. Don't forget it!

# 📕 PART 4: The Building (Chapter 4: Demonstratives)

Nexora's building was huge, made of glass. Yasmine stopped in front of it.

"**This** is the main entrance," said Adam, pointing at the door next to him. "And **that** is the parking lot over there."

"**These** doors are amazing," Yasmine said, touching the glass. "And look at **those** screens on the top floor. They show the stock prices of Microsoft, Google and Amazon."

Inside, she met a man in a gray suit. "**This** is Mr. Karim, the HR manager," said the receptionist. "And **that** is the CEO's office, over there."

Mr. Karim smiled. "I hope **this** water is cold enough for you. **These** chairs are comfortable, I promise."

Yasmine remembered her mistakes in the past. "**That** interview two years ago was a disaster," she thought. "But **this** one will be different. **This** situation is stressful, but **that** experience taught me a lot."

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 4: Demonstratives | أسماء الإشارة

| | **Near** (قريب) | **Far** (بعيد) |
|---|---|---|
| Adverb | **here** | **there** |
| Singular / uncountable | **this** (this door, this water) | **that** (that office) |
| Plural | **these** (these chairs) | **those** (those screens) |

**4 ways to use them:** before a noun (*These doors*) • before adjective + noun (*That old man*) • before **one/ones** (*this one*) • alone (*Those aren't yours*).

- **Events:** `this/these` = present / now → *This situation is stressful.* | `that/those` = past / finished → *That interview was a disaster.*
- **Introducing people:** "**This** is Mr. Karim."
- **Memory trick:** this/these → "S" → close to **S**peaker. that/those → "T" → far.
- ❌ ~~This tickets~~ → ✅ **These** tickets. ❌ ~~These water~~ → ✅ **This** water.

# 📒 PART 5: Waiting for the CEO (Chapter 5: all / half)

Mr. Karim told Yasmine: "**All** candidates wait here before the interview. **All of them** are excellent."

Yasmine looked around. There were twenty people in the room. **Half of them** were from Europe and **half of the** group came from Asia. "**All of us** are nervous," she whispered.

On the table there was **a** big jar of cookies. "Have you eaten **all the** cookies?" Adam asked a boy. "**Half of the** cookies are gone already!"

The boy laughed. "Look at **all these** people! **All this** stress for one job!"

Mr. Karim said: "The interview will take **half an hour**. The CEO's coffee machine uses **half a kilo** of coffee every week! **All the** money for the new offices comes from our investors. **All parents** want the best for their children, and **all employees** at Nexora want the best for the company."

"**Half of the** candidates **were** nervous," Yasmine noticed, "and **half of the** coffee **was** gone."

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 5: all / half | كل / نصف

**ALL** (100%):
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| all + plural/uncountable (general) | **All parents** want the best. |
| all (of) + the/my/… + noun (specific) | **all (of) the cookies** |
| all **of** + pronoun (**of** required) | **all of us**, **all of them** (object pronouns!) |
| all (of) + this/these (questions & exclamations) | Look at **all this** snow! / **all these** people! |

**HALF** (50%):
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| half + a/an + measurement | **half an hour**, **half a kilo** |
| half **of** + pronoun | **half of us** |
| half (of) + the/a/this/my + noun | **half the** people, **half of that** cake |

> ✅ **The verb follows the noun after "of":** *Half of the **students were** present* (plural) / *Half of the **cake was** eaten* (singular).
> ❌ ~~Half parents~~ → ✅ Half **of the** parents. ❌ ~~All us~~ → ✅ All **of us**. ❌ ~~half miles~~ → ✅ half **a mile**.

# 📔 PART 6: The Interview Starts (Chapter 6: each / every)

Finally, a door opened. **Every** candidate stood up. The CEO, Ms. Laura Bennett, entered.

"Welcome," she said. "**Each of you** has a different story. **Every** person in this room has a talent. **Each** candidate will meet me for ten minutes."

"We **each** have a CV," Adam whispered to Yasmine (he was waiting for her). "**Each of us** is a little afraid."

The CEO continued: "**Every** morning I read the news. **Every** time a new technology appears, I ask my team: *'How can this help our customers?'* **Each of the** engineers on my team gets a laptop, and we pay a bonus of $500 **each**."

Mr. Karim told Yasmine: "**Every** employee at Nexora has a mentor. We use **every** chance to learn. And remember, **to each** his own: **each** person works differently."

Yasmine's two friends in the room **each** have their own start-up. "**Every** boy and girl here dreams big," she smiled.

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 6: each / every | كل واحد / كل

| | **each** (one by one) | **every** (all, no exception) |
|---|---|---|
| + singular noun | ✅ each candidate | ✅ every person |
| + plural | ✅ **each of** the engineers | ❌ ~~every engineers~~ |
| + pronoun | ✅ each **of us** | ❌ ~~every of us~~ |
| after the subject | ✅ We **each** have a CV | ❌ |
| end of sentence | ✅ $500 **each** | ❌ |
| time | less common | ✅ **every** morning, **every time** |

- Both: **countable singular noun + singular verb** (*Each person **is**…*).
- ❌ ~~Every boys~~ → ✅ **Every boy**. ❌ ~~Each the pupils~~ → ✅ Each **of** the pupils.
- Idiom: **To each his own** (لكلٍّ ذوقه) — modern: *to each their own*.

# 📓 PART 7: Who Is She? (Chapter 7: Verb Conjugation & "to be")

"Please introduce yourself," said Ms. Bennett.

"**I am** Yasmine. **I'm** twenty-four years old. **I am** an IT engineer from Tunis. **You are** very kind to receive me. **We are** all curious about your vision. **It is** an honor."

The CEO smiled. "**He is** my assistant, **she is** my director, and **they are** my team. When **I was** your age, **I was** very shy. **You were** brave to come today. **We were** all afraid at the beginning!"

"When **I was** a student, **we were** only five girls in the class," said Yasmine. "**They were** the best days of my life. **It was** difficult, but **I'm** glad."

"You must **be** kidding!" laughed the CEO. "**We have been** in this field for ages and **it is** still difficult."

"How **is** this possible?" Yasmine asked. "**These jeans** (her old pair!) **are** too tight, and **the Potts** (her neighbors) **are** attending the ceremony. **They are** my friends."

> 🌍 In Arabic we say "أنا مهندسة" (without a verb). In English you **must** write: **I am** an engineer. ✅ ~~I an engineer~~ ❌.

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 7: Persons & "to be" | الأشخاص وفعل الكينونة

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** (المتكلم) | I | we |
| **2nd** (المخاطب) | you | you |
| **3rd** (الغائب) | he / she / it | they |

| Person | Present (singular / plural) | Past (singular / plural) |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | I **am** / we **are** | I **was** / we **were** |
| 2nd | you **are** / you **are** | you **were** / you **were** |
| 3rd | he, she, it **is** / they **are** | he, she, it **was** / they **were** |

- **Short forms:** I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're, isn't, aren't, wasn't, weren't.
- **Other forms:** **be** (after a modal: *must **be***), **been** (after have: *have **been***), **being** (continuous: *is **being** silly*).
- **Find the person:** replace the subject with a pronoun → *The Potts* = **they**, *These jeans* = **they**, *Sarah* = **she**.
- ⚠️ ❌ ~~you was~~ → ✅ **you were**. ❌ ~~we was~~ → ✅ **we were**.

# 📘 PART 8: Yasmine's Story (Chapter 8: Past Simple)

Ms. Bennett asked: "Tell me about your past. How **did** you **get** into IT?"

Yasmine took a deep breath.

"**I grew up** in Tunis. When **I was** a teenager, **I ran** every day and **I played** video games at night. **I was** beyond happy when **I got** my first computer. **My dad bought** it for me in 2012. **I opened** the box, **I plugged** it in and **I started** to learn programming. **I tried** a hundred times and **I cried** twice, but **I never stopped**.

Last year **I went** to a conference in Germany. **I drove** to the airport, **I took** a plane and **I saw** the Alps from the sky. **I met** engineers from Google and Amazon. They **gave** me a card and **I wrote** my name on a list. **I knew** this was my chance. Two years ago **I didn't have** enough money for the trip, so **I signed up** for a scholarship. **I didn't want** to stay at home. **I didn't give up**."

"And yesterday?" asked the CEO. "**Did** you **sleep** well?"

"**No, I didn't.** **I didn't sleep** at all! **I made** a coffee, **I wrote** my speech, **I ate** an apple and **I fell** asleep at 4 a.m. Last night **I said** to myself: *'Where were you when your dream called?'* — **I was** here!"

Ms. Bennett laughed. "**Did** you **study** hard?"

"Yes! **I studied** every night when **I was** a student. And **I told** my friends: *'We didn't invite Ann to the party, but we invited our books!'* Then **I left** the house and **I came** here."

> ⏰ **Time markers in this part:** *in 2012, last year, two years ago, yesterday, last night, when I was a teenager, every day.*

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 8: Past Simple | الماضي البسيط

**Uses:** finished action at a **definite time** (*in 2012*) • finished action (*I lived in Paris*) • **how someone felt** (*I was beyond happy*) • **past habits** (*I ran every day*).

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| (+) | subject + **past form** | I **went** to Germany. |
| (−) | subject + **didn't** + **base verb** | I **didn't have** money. |
| (?) | **Did** + subject + **base verb**? | **Did** you **sleep**? |
| Wh? | Wh + **did** + subject + base? | **How did** you **get** in? |

**Regular spelling:** +ed (*want → wanted*) • ends in e → +d (*like → liked*) • consonant + y → **ied** (*try → tried, cry → cried, study → studied*) • 1 vowel + 1 consonant → double (*stop → stopped*) • vowel + y → +ed (*play → played*).
**Pronouncing -ed:** /ɪd/ after t/d (*wanted*) • /t/ (*worked*) • /d/ (*played*).

**Irregular verbs in the story:**

| Present | Past | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | go | went |
| get | got | buy | bought |
| run | ran | drive | drove |
| take | took | see | saw |
| meet | met | give | gave |
| write | wrote | know | knew |
| make | made | eat | ate |
| fall | fell | say | said |
| tell | told | leave | left |
| come | came | grow | grew |

> ⚠️ **Golden rule:** after `did / didn't` the verb goes back to the **base**. ✅ *Did you **go**?* ❌ ~~Did you went?~~ / ✅ *I didn't **invite** her.* ❌ ~~I didn't invited~~.
> ⚠️ **to be** → **no did**: ✅ *Were you angry?* / *I wasn't angry.* ❌ ~~Did you were?~~
> ⚠️ ✅ *last night* ❌ ~~the last night~~. ✅ *Kim **went*** ❌ ~~Kim goed~~.

# 📗 PART 9: A Day at Nexora (Chapter 9: Present Simple)

"Now tell me about your daily life," said Ms. Bennett.

"**I wake up** at six. **I always drink** coffee, and **I usually eat** a light breakfast. **I often work** from seven to five. **I sometimes go** hiking on Saturday, and **I never skip** my morning run. I **don't use** social media at work. I **like** painting and reading."

"And your brother?"

"**Adam studies** computer science. **He doesn't live** in Tunis now. **He watches** videos about AI every day. **He likes** everything the old-fashioned way. **He never goes** to bed early. **He is always** in a hurry, and **he is rarely** late."

"Our work is similar," said the CEO. "**Our shift begins** at 8 a.m. **The company opens** at seven and **closes** at six. **Does** your brother **work** with you?"

"No, he **doesn't**. **Does** he **know** about Nexora? Yes, he **does**. **Where does** he **come** from? He **comes** from Tunis, like me."

"And what time **do** you **go** to bed?"

"I **go** to bed at ten. **I rarely stay** up late. **She** (my mother) **watches** TV and **my father studies** the news. **The sun rises** in the east."

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 9: Present Simple | المضارع البسيط

**Uses:** habits & routines • general truths • facts/timetables • likes & opinions.

| Type | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| (+) | I **work** | he **works** |
| (−) | I **don't work** | he **doesn't work** |
| (?) | **Do** you **work**? | **Does** he **work**? |

**Spelling for he / she / it:** most verbs +**s** (*likes*) • o, s, ch, sh, x (z) → +**es** (*watches, does, goes*) • consonant + y → **ies** (*studies, cries*) • vowel + y → +s (*plays*).
**Irregular:** *to be* → am / is / are • *to have* → have / **has**. (BrE: *I have got a car* = AmE: *I have a car*.)

> ⚠️ With `does / doesn't` the main verb has **NO -s**. ✅ *Does she **work**?* ❌ ~~Does she works?~~ / ✅ *He doesn't **use** it.*

**Adverbs of frequency:** always (100%) • usually (~80%) • often (~60%) • sometimes (~40%) • rarely (~10%) • never (0%).
- **Before** normal verbs: *I **always drink** coffee.*
- **After** the verb **to be**: *He **is always** in a hurry.*
- ❌ ~~He always is~~ in a hurry. ❌ ~~What time you go~~ to bed? → ✅ What time **do you go**?

# 📙 PART 10: The Team Meeting (Chapter 10: Subject-Verb Agreement)

After the interview, Ms. Bennett invited Yasmine to a team meeting. **Liz is** an accountant and **she has** a typical 8–5 job. **She cooks** dinner, and **her brothers make** breakfast.

"**Everyone is** here," said Mr. Karim. "**Nobody masters** a project without making mistakes. **Each of these suggestions is** interesting. **Someone was standing** at the door, but **no one knows** who."

"**The team is** ready," said the CEO. "**The group meets** every other week. **The jury has** reached a decision about the best idea. **My family is** very big, but **our staff is** bigger!"

The IT manager added: "**The CEO and the CFO are** in the building. **A car and a bike are** my means of transportation. **Either your mother or your dad needs** to sign the form. **The teacher or the students write** on the board. **The students or the teacher writes** on the board. **Neither Sarah nor I am** going to the party. **I, as well as my colleagues, am** excited."

"**There is** a laptop on the table," said Adam. "**There are** many laptops in the room. **There is a lot of** noise and **there are a lot of** people! **Ten kilometers is** too far to walk, and **ten dollars is** a high price for a mouse. But **ten dollars were** scattered on the floor (separate bills). **These jeans are** too revealing for the office, and **the Potts are** here too."

> 📝 **The aim of the research was** to find life on Mars. (subject = **aim**, singular).

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 10: Subject-Verb Agreement | مطابقة الفعل للفاعل

The verb must agree with its subject in **person** and **number**.

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **and** → PLURAL | *The CEO **and** the CFO **are***. |
| 2 | everyone, nobody, each, someone, no one → SINGULAR | *Everyone **is** here.* |
| 3 | **or / nor** + singular subjects → SINGULAR | *Either your mother or dad **needs***. |
| 4 | **Proximity:** verb agrees with the CLOSEST subject | *The teacher or **the students write*** / *The students or **the teacher writes*** / *Neither Sarah nor **I am*** |
| 5 | **There is / There are** → agrees with the noun AFTER | *There **is** a laptop. / There **are** laptops.* |
| 6 | a lot of / some / all + **of** + noun → agrees with that noun | *There is **a lot of fuss** / There are **a lot of people*** |
| 7 | **Money, time, distance** as ONE unit → SINGULAR | *Ten kilometers **is** too far.* |
| 8 | **Collective nouns** (family, team, group, jury, staff, class) → SINGULAR | *The team **is** ready.* (BrE: sometimes plural) |

> ⚠️ "**as well as**" and "**together with**" do **not** make the subject plural: *I, as well as my colleagues, **am** excited.*
> ❌ ~~My cousins and my brother doesn't~~ → ✅ **don't**. ❌ ~~Nobody master~~ → ✅ **masters**. ❌ ~~There is many people~~ → ✅ **are**.

# 📕 PART 11: The Offer (Chapter 11: Future Simple with will)

At the end of the day, Ms. Bennett stood up and shook Yasmine's hand.

"**I will** make a decision tomorrow," she said. "**Perhaps** you'**ll** get the job. **You'll probably** hear from us in two days. We **definitely won't** forget you."

Yasmine thought about the future. "Technology **will** change the world. **Robots won't** take over the planet, but they'**ll** help us. **I don't think** machines **will** replace us. **Things will** happen on their own. **Time will tell**."

"**I promise** I'**ll** work hard," she told the CEO. "**I'll** do all my tasks on time. **I won't** be late. **We'll try** to make it work. **We'll let** you know my answer. **I'll pay** for your coffee!"

"**Shall we** go to the cafeteria?" asked the CEO. "**Shall I** show you the building?"

"**Will you** hire me?" Yasmine asked with a smile.

"**Will** I? Next week **you'll** receive a contract. **Max will certainly** call you **later tonight** (he is the HR assistant). **Susan will try** to come **next month**, but she **probably won't** have time. **Where will** you live? **Will** you **move** to a new city?"

"**I'll probably** stay in Tunis. Or **I'll** go to **the** U.S. **I'll** let my parents sleep at my place and **I'll** pay for the meal."

That night, Yasmine looked at the stars. "**Tomorrow**, **next week**, **next year**… **I will** be a great engineer," she whispered. "**I won't** stop. **I will** continue."

And the next day, the email arrived:

> *"Dear Yasmine, welcome to Nexora. You will start on Monday. — Laura Bennett, CEO"*

**THE END** 🏆

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher's Box 11: Future Simple (will) | المستقبل البسيط

**Uses:** predictions • beliefs & opinions • hopes & promises • spontaneous decisions / offers (*I'll pay*).

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| (+) | subject + **will** + base verb | She **will come**. / She'**ll** come. |
| (−) | subject + **won't** + base verb | She **won't come**. |
| (?) | **Will** + subject + base verb? | **Will** she **come**? |
| Wh? | Wh + **will** + subject + base? | **Where will** you **live**? |

- **Same form for ALL persons.** After will: **no -s, no -ing, no to**. ❌ ~~She will goes / will to go~~.
- **Contractions:** I'll, you'll, he'll, she'll, it'll, we'll, they'll, won't.
- **Time markers:** tomorrow, tonight, next week/month/year, later, in two days.
- **Certainty:** definitely / certainly (🔵🔵🔵) • probably (🔵🔵) • perhaps / maybe (🔵).
- **Position:** positive → **after will** (*We'll **probably** do it*) • negative → **before won't** (*We **probably** won't do it*) • *perhaps* → start of sentence.
- **shall** → suggestions with I / we: ✅ *Shall we go?* ❌ ~~Shall you go?~~
- ✅ *I **don't think** robots **will** take over* (more natural) ❌ ~~I think robots won't…~~
