## 2. Part A: `ALL` (كل / جميع)

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
`all` talks about the **whole group**, with emphasis that **nothing has been left out**.
> ***All** people want to love and to be loved.*

### 📊 The 4 Patterns of `all` | أنماط all الأربعة

| # | Pattern | النمط | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **all + plural / uncountable noun** | عام | **All parents** want the best for their children. | The group as a **concept** (in general) |
| 2 | **all (of) + the / possessive + noun** | محدد | Have you eaten **all (of) the cookies** in the jar? | A **specific, physical** group |
| 3 | **all of + plural pronoun** | مع الضمير | **All of us** are going to be there. | `of` is **required** |
| 4 | **all (of) + this/that/these/those + noun** (questions & exclamations) | تعجب/سؤال | Look at **all this snow**! / What are **all these people** doing here? | Surprise / strong feeling |

> 💡 **`of` is optional** in patterns 2 and 4 (*all the cookies* = *all of the cookies*) but **required** in pattern 3 (*all of us*, never ~~all us~~).
> 🔗 **Link to Chapter 2:** after `of` we use **object pronouns**: *all of **us**, all of **you**, all of **them***.

### 🧠 General vs. Specific | عام أم محدد؟

| General (no `the`) | Specific (with `the` / my / these…) |
|---|---|
| **All parents** love their kids. *(parents in the world)* | **All the parents** at school came. *(those parents)* |
| **All money** is useful. *(money in general)* | **All the money** went into the preparation. *(that money)* |

## 3. Part B: `HALF` (نصف)

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
`half` talks about a whole group **divided in two**.

### 📊 The 3 Patterns of `half` | أنماط half الثلاثة

| # | Pattern | النمط | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **half + a/an + measurement noun** | قياس | I'll be back in **half an hour**. / **half a kilo** of sugar |
| 2 | **half of + plural pronoun** | مع الضمير | Only **half of us** are going. |
| 3 | **half (of) + the / a / this-that / possessive + noun** | محدد | **Half the people** left. / I want **half of that cake**! / I used **half of your eggs**. |

> 💡 `of` is **optional** in pattern 3 (*half the people* = *half of the people*) but **required** with pronouns (*half of **us***).

---

## 4. ✅ Subject-Verb Agreement | مطابقة الفعل

The verb agrees with the **noun after `of`**, not with `half` or `all`.

| Noun type | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Plural | **are / were** | **Half of the students were** present. / Only half of the reviews **are** positive. |
| Singular / uncountable | **is / was** | **Half of the cake was** eaten. / **All the money was** spent. |

## 5. 🔍 `all` vs. `half`: Side by Side | مقارنة سريعة

| | **all** | **half** |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | 100% | 50% |
| + pronoun | **all of** us/you/them | **half of** us/you/them |
| + the + noun | all (of) **the** cookies | half (of) **the** cookies |
| + this/that | all (of) **this** snow | half (of) **that** cake |
| + measurement | ❌ | half **a/an** hour, half **a** kilo |
| Alone + plural/uncountable | ✅ **All** parents… | ❌ ~~Half parents~~ |

## 7. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
ALL   → all + plural/uncountable         All parents…            (general)
      → all (of) + the/my/…  + noun      all (of) the cookies    (specific)
      → all OF + pronoun                 all of us               (of REQUIRED)
      → all (of) + this/these + noun     all this snow!          (exclamation)

HALF  → half + a/an + measure            half an hour, half a kilo
      → half OF + pronoun                half of us              (of REQUIRED)
      → half (of) + the/a/this/my + noun half the people, half of that cake

VERB follows the noun after "of":  Half of the students WERE… / Half of the cake WAS…
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| **All parents** want **their** kids to be happy. | All parents want ~~his~~ kids… |
| Think of **all the** money! | Think of ~~all money~~ *(here the money is specific)*! |
| **Half of the students were** present. | Half of the students ~~was~~ present. |
| Look at **these** cute shoes! | Look at ~~this~~ cute shoes! |
| He runs **half a mile** a day. | He runs ~~half miles~~ a day. |
| **All of us** are going. | ~~All us~~ are going. |

## 8. Practice with Answers | تمارين مع الحلول

### Exercise 1: Find the mistakes

| # | Wrong sentence | Correction | Why? |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | My dad runs half ~~miles~~ a day. | half **a mile** | `half` + **a/an** + singular measurement |
| b | All parents want ~~his~~ kids… | **their** kids | `parents` is plural → `their` |
| c | Think of all ~~money~~… | all **the** money | specific money (that went into the preparation) |
| d | Half of the students ~~was~~… | **were** | `students` is plural |
| e | Look at ~~this~~ cute shoes! | **these** | `shoes` is plural |

### Exercise 2: Match the sentences

| Sentence | Match |
|---|---|
| a. Half of them ignored our dinner party. | **5.** That was very disrespectful of them. |
| b. You'd better not eat this burger. | **4.** Think of all the calories! |
| c. Look at all these balloons! | **1.** Are they having a party over there? |
| d. Where are all the apples? | **2.** I bought a kilo yesterday! |
| e. I don't know if this movie is worth seeing. | **3.** Only half of the reviews are positive. |

> **Teacher's note:** In 1c, `all money` isn't always wrong (*All money is useful* is fine in general). It needs **the** here only because the sentence refers to **specific** money ("that went into the preparation").
