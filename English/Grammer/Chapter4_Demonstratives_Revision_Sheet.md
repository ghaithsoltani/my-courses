## 2. Part A: The Four Demonstratives | أسماء الإشارة الأربعة

### 📌 Definition | التعريف
Demonstratives are words that show **which** person or thing we mean, and **where** it is in relation to the speaker.
أسماء الإشارة تحدد **أي** شخص أو شيء نقصد، وبُعده أو قربه من المتكلم.

> ***This** is Hugh, and **that** is Kevin.*

### 📊 The Table | الجدول

| | **Near** the speaker (قريب) | **Far** from the speaker (بعيد) |
|---|---|---|
| **Adverbs** (ظروف) | **here** (هنا) | **there** (هناك) |
| **Singular & uncountable** (مفرد / غير معدود) | **this** (هذا/هذه) | **that** (ذلك/تلك) |
| **Plural countable** (جمع معدود) | **these** (هؤلاء) | **those** (أولئك) |

### 🧠 Memory Trick | حيلة للحفظ

```
THIS  →  ONE  + NEAR      (this book)       THESE → MANY + NEAR   (these books)
THAT  →  ONE  + FAR       (that book)       THOSE → MANY + FAR    (those books)

T-H-I-S / T-H-E-S-E  → "S" sound → close to S-peaker   (near)
T-H-A-T / T-H-O-S-E  → "T" → far away                   (far)
```

## 3. Part B: How to Use Them | طرق الاستعمال

| # | Use | الاستعمال | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Before a noun** | قبل الاسم | **These** oranges are delicious! |
| 2 | **Before adjective + noun** | قبل الصفة والاسم | **That** old man stole my purse! |
| 3 | **Before "one/ones"** (noun understood) | قبل one/ones | I'll take **this one**, please. (= this watermelon) |
| 4 | **Alone** (noun understood) | وحدها بدون اسم | **Those** aren't yours. Put them back. (= those shoes) |

> 💡 In English the demonstrative comes **before** the noun. (In Arabic, it often comes first too: *هذا الكتاب* → **this** book.)

### ⏳ Time: present vs. past | الزمن: الحاضر والماضي
When talking about **events**:
- **this / these** → the **present** (or something happening now)
- **that / those** → the **past** (or something finished)

> ***This** situation is quite unstable.* → now
> ***That** event made me realise how important my family is.* → in the past

### ❤️ Physical vs. Psychological distance | المسافة المادية والنفسية
Distance can be **emotional**, not only physical.
> *I can't understand why they did **this** to her.* → close, emotionally involved
> *I want something like **that**.* → far/not here, just described

## 4. 🔍 Quick Comparison | مقارنة سريعة

| Sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **This** dress is beautiful. | The dress is **near** me (in my hands / next to me) |
| **That** dress is beautiful. | The dress is **far** (over there / across the room) |
| **These** shoes are comfortable. | Plural, **near** |
| **Those** shoes are expensive. | Plural, **far** |

## 6. ⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet | ورقة الغش السريعة

```
         NEAR        FAR
Adverb   here        there
Singular this        that       (also uncountable: this water, that music)
Plural   these       those

• Before a noun:        These oranges…  /  That old man…
• Before "one/ones":    this one, those ones
• Alone (noun known):   Those aren't yours.
• Events:               this = present   |   that = past
• Introducing people:   "This is Hugh."  |  Pointing far: "That is Kevin."
```

### ✅ Do & ❌ Don't

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| **These** tickets are expensive. | ~~This~~ tickets are expensive. |
| **This** movie is my favourite. | ~~These~~ movie is my favourite. |
| **Those** shoes aren't yours. | ~~That~~ shoes aren't yours. |
| **This** water is cold. *(uncountable)* | ~~These~~ water is cold. |
| **That** is my teacher over there. | ~~Those~~ is my teacher over there. |

## 7. Practice with Answers | تمارين مع الحلول

**Fill in the gaps with `this` or `these`.**

| # | Sentence | Answer | Why? |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | Why are you scared? – ___! (pointing at a snake) | **This** | one snake, near |
| b | Which bags are yours? – ___ ones. | **These** | plural, near |
| c | You are always late. – ___ is not true! | **This** | the statement just said |
| d | Harry Potter? ___ movie is my favourite! | **This** | singular |
| e | ___ people are boring! | **These** | plural |
| f | Is ___ Mr. Jones? – No, ___ is Mr. Adams. | **this … this** | introducing someone near |
| g | ___ is for me? I'm so touched! | **This** | singular, near |
| h | ___ tickets are so expensive! | **These** | plural |
| i | ___ is unbelievable… why they did ___ to her. | **This … this** | singular things |
| j | ___ are the books you asked for. | **These** | plural, near |

> **Teacher's note:** In the worksheet, the last item is labelled **"g"** twice. It should be **"j"**. Also, in item **f**, because Mr. Jones is *over there*, a native speaker might use **that** for him. The key uses **this** for the person right next to you.
